How posting works now

Your app can really publish to Instagram. Here is exactly what happens, and where it stops.

The Meta developer route is no longer needed. Everything that page described — a Facebook app, App Review, long-lived tokens, refreshing them every 60 days — is replaced by the Postproxy connector on your Claude account. It already holds the Instagram permission, so the app borrows it at the moment you tap Approve. No token ever touches this app or me.

The account

Confirmed: posts go to your connected profile. That is the profile Postproxy has connected, and you have confirmed it is the right one. The app has it saved as the expected handle, so the orange mismatch warning is gone. It still shows the live account name and id on the Schedule tab and on every approval screen — if it ever stops saying your connected profile, something changed in Postproxy, and you should not approve until you know why.

What happens, step by step

1

You schedule it

Schedule tab: pick story, post or reel, give the public https address of the photo or video, set a time. It is saved, not sent.

2

The clock checks every hour

A scheduled task runs on Anthropic's servers, with your phone closed. It can only move a due action to Waiting for you. It holds no Instagram access at all, so it cannot post even if something told it to.

3

You get a notification

Approvals tab shows the post with a red badge on the tab bar.

4

You read exactly what will go out

The account name and id, the format, the caption, the media address, and the precise arguments that will be sent — plus a SHA-256 fingerprint of all of it. There is a Check it first button that asks Postproxy to validate the post without publishing it.

5

You tap Publish now

Only then does anything leave. The app re-checks the connected account one last time; if it changed since you opened the screen, it refuses and posts nothing.

6

The result is recorded honestly

Posted, Not posted with the reason, or Outcome unknown if Postproxy never answered. It is never marked posted unless Postproxy said it was.

Things that will trip you up

The media must be a public linkInstagram fetches the file itself, so https://…/photo.jpg works and a photo in your camera roll does not. JPEG for photos.
Stories carry no captionInstagram's API does not accept one, so the app sends an empty caption for a story. Whatever you type is just a note to yourself.
No link or poll stickersNot available through any API.
A daily cap appliesInstagram limits API posts per 24 hours. Normal personal use will not reach it.
Approval covers one post, onceThe fingerprint is sent as the idempotency key, so the same approved post cannot double up.

What still cannot happen

  • I cannot post for you. Only the app can, and only from your tap.
  • The hourly scheduler cannot post. It is created with no connectors, so it has no route to Instagram.
  • Nothing posts silently. There is no setting to skip approval, by design.
  • Nothing is unsendable. Once a story or post is live, it is live. That is why the screen is red.
